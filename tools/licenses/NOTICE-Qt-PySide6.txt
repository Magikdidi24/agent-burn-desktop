Agent Burn uses these libraries unmodified, as published on PyPI (packages
PySide6-Essentials and shiboken6 @PYSIDE6@). They remain separate shared libraries, loaded at
run time from the application's PySide6/ folder (and the lib*.so.6 links at its root on Linux):
they can be replaced by a compatible modified version without recompiling the application
(LGPL-3.0, section 4 d) 1)).

Corresponding sources:
  Qt @QT@: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/
  PySide6 and Shiboken6 @PYSIDE6@: https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/
    (repository: https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git, tag v@PYSIDE6@)

Third-party components compiled into the shipped Qt and PySide6 files (ICU on Linux, HarfBuzz, PCRE2, libpng…):
list, licenses and texts in QT-THIRD-PARTY.txt. For all of Qt: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html
